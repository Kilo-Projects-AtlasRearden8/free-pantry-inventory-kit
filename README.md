# Free Pantry Inventory Kit

A free, printable, fillable pantry management kit from **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**.

No account needed. No tracking. No ads. Just open the files in your browser, fill them in, and print.

## What is in the kit

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | A single printable HTML page with all four tools, fillable in your browser |
| `pantry-kit.md` | The same four tools in Markdown, for notes, copy-paste, or paper |

## The four tools

1. **Pantry Stock Tracker** — track what you have, where it lives, how much, and when it expires. Use FIFO (first in, first out) so older items get used first.
2. **Grocery Master List** — a reusable, categorized master list of items to buy. Check off what you need, then rebuild the list next time.
3. **Price Book** — log prices per store so you can compare unit prices and find the best deal. Lower price per unit wins.
4. **Meal Staples Checklist** — a kitchen staples audit so you always have the building blocks for quick meals on hand.

## How to use it

1. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.
2. Fill in the fields. Everything saves in your browser only.
3. When you are ready to print, press **Ctrl+P** (or Cmd+P on Mac) and choose a printer.

For a paper copy, print first, then walk your pantry with a pen.

## Local-first and private

Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and no account is required. This kit is designed for low-energy, ADHD-friendly life admin: keep it simple, repeat it weekly, and let the list do the remembering for you.
